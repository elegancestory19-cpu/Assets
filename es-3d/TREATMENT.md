# ELEGANCE STORY: «مدينة Elegance Story» (Low-poly Isometric Island)

A second film for the shop. Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, **about 36 s** (final length follows the client's recording). Voice-over in Sétif darja, recorded by the client (7 short lines). Arabic captions and an `.srt`. Master at −14 LUFS.

**Client rules that override the style:**
- **No SFX at all.** The style asks for foley (splashes, knocks, hums), but this client rejects it. Every arrival lands on a **score note** played on a real recorded instrument (marimba, kalimba, glockenspiel, vibraphone, darbuka), so *building is still composing*.
- **Real instruments only.** No synth pad, no noise beds. The bowed vibraphone holds the long notes.
- **The voice is not processed**: no compression, EQ, speed or pitch change. Silences are trimmed only; the music ducks ≥ 12 dB under every word.
- **The official logo only**, as an image on surfaces (panels, a T-shirt, a mug, the sign, the tower screen). It may move, scale or fade, and is never redrawn or recoloured. **This is the only texture in the film** (the style forbids textures; the brand rule wins). **No other brands.**
- **Safe zone:** key action stays between y 180 and 1540 and left of x 940.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Growth.** One island grows from a single tile into the whole shop as each service is added. *Too close to the style's demo idea (an island that grows) and its natural cliché.*
2. **Erosion in reverse (the variation space's "erosion").** The finished town loses pieces until only the logo is left. *Strong ending, but it starts on the answer and has no customer in it.*
3. **One order's journey, in one take (chosen).** A tiny glowing cube (the customer's *idea*) rises out of the sea and travels across a chain of small islands. Each island is one service and rises out of the water as the idea arrives. The idea is drawn, printed on glass, wood and metal, pressed onto a T-shirt, a cap and a mug, and enlarged onto a facade, as day turns to night. Then a pure zoom-out shows the five islands are one town, and every window lights at once: «كلّش تحت سقف واحد».

**Why 3:** it is a story with a protagonist (the customer's idea) and a turn (from a lone speck at dawn to a lit town at night). It uses four native moves where the story needs them: *rise from below* for each island, *building is composing* (every arrival is a note), *light as clock* (dawn → night) and *emissive blocks in the dark* (the town lighting up). The opening is "the smallest thing first", the ending a scale reveal at night; neither is the demo's.

## 2. Logline and arc

*A customer's idea, a glowing cube at dawn, crosses five tiny islands, each a service of Elegance Story, and at nightfall the camera pulls back to show they were one town, lit under one roof.*

- **Setup** (0–3.5 s): one speck of light on an empty pastel sea at dawn. «عندك فكرة؟»
- **Journey** (3.5–25 s): design → UV print → gifts → facade and sign, while the sun moves from morning to dusk.
- **Turn** (25 s): night falls; for one beat only the idea glows.
- **Reveal** (25–31 s): a pure orthographic zoom-out; windows light one per note, and the logo screen on the central tower lights last.
- **End** (31–36 s): a text card over the darkened town (the world stays visible around it), with the logo and the contact details.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Townscaper**: building as performance. *Learn*: every piece lands with a springy bounce and a sound; pieces sprout small details by themselves. *Don't take*: its grid, buildings or colours.
- **Monument Valley**: isometric calm. *Learn*: pastel gradients, empty space, a patient camera. *Don't take*: its architecture, characters or type.
- **Lumière-style "day into night" time-lapse sequences in animation**: light as clock. *Learn*: keyframed sky colours, and warm emissives as the only bright things at night.

## 4. Look

- **World**: pointy-top hex tiles (sand at the water line, a soft mint grass, a warm stone), box buildings with prism roofs, a few cone and icosahedron trees. Flat-shaded, roughness ≈ 0.9, lightness jittered per tile.
- **Brand colours as the wall and roof set**, softened to pastel: cyan, blue, purple, magenta, orange and yellow from the logo, each lifted toward white. **The idea cube is saturated brand cyan `#1CCEE9`**, emissive, the one fully saturated object. At night the warm windows and the logo screen join it.
- **Sea**: faceted pastel water, deep → shallow near land, a thin foam rim.
- **Sky** (time-of-day keys): dawn pale peach → day airy blue → dusk apricot and lilac → night blue-violet, with stars only in thick haze.
- **Type**: Cairo 700 for captions (no box, warm white, soft dark-blue drop shadow, a small hex icon whose top colour follows the time of day). Cairo 800 for the end card. Numbers and the email are isolated LTR runs.

## 7. Cue map

**112 BPM** (beat 0.536 s), 4/4, D major pentatonic (D E F# A B): bright and consonant in any order.

| Section | Music | Picture hits |
|---|---|---|
| 1 dawn | one glockenspiel note (**the idea's motif**: D6) on the rise; a bowed-vibraphone D drone | the cube settles on the note |
| 2 design | kalimba plays one note per rising tile (eighths); a marimba ostinato enters | each tile, studio, board |
| 3 print | + darbuka (doum / tak), a light groove; the lamp passes as a vibraphone run, and each panel is one marimba note | the panels, on beats |
| 4 gifts | a pattern on marimba + kalimba; the lid drops on a bass note (jazz double bass) | the lid; the three objects on beats |
| 5 facade | floors stack as rising marimba notes (an additive build); dusk thins it to the bass and the bowed vib | each floor; the sign switching on |
| 6 night | **one beat of digital zero** before the zoom; then the windows play the whole melody in spatial order (building is composing), and the motif returns an octave lower and full on the logo screen | each window, then the screen |
| 7 end | bowed-vib D major add9; the motif once more, softly; ring out to silence | the end-card rows |
