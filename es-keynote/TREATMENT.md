# ELEGANCE STORY: «كلّش تحت سقف واحد» (Dark Tech Keynote)

A full introduction to the shop. Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, **45.0 s**. Voice-over in Sétif darja, recorded by the client (script and timings below). Burned-in Arabic captions and an `.srt`. Master at −14 LUFS.

**Client rules that override the style:**
- **No SFX at all.** The style asks for foley, but this client rejects it. Every on-screen action lands on a **score note** played on a real recorded instrument (marimba, vibraphone, glockenspiel), so the picture still sounds right with no "sound effects".
- **Real instruments only.** No synth pad, sine pulse or plucked synth. The bowed vibraphone takes the pad's job.
- Music ducks **≥ 12 dB** under every word (the style says 8).
- **Official logo only.** It may move, scale or fade, and is never redrawn or recoloured. **No other brands:** the UI kit is original, with no OS chrome and no app logos.
- **Safe zone:** content stays between y 180 and 1540 and left of x 940. The centre of the content column is x 500.
- The on-screen words are **Arabic** (Cairo). Mono labels (JetBrains Mono) are used only for numbers and codes: `01/12`, `3.20 m`, `60×90`, `CMYK+W+V`, `4K`.

---

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A zoom ladder.** One printed pixel → a business card → a T-shirt → a 3.20 m facade → a whole street at night. Each step up in scale is one service. *Strong scale idea, but it can't hold design, video and web, which have no physical size.*
2. **A release checklist (countdown).** A single customer order ticks through a checklist: design ✓, print ✓, cut ✓, press ✓, install ✓, "delivered". *Clear, but it tells one order's story, not the shop's range, and reads as a process film.*
3. **Storm → one tile → snap grid (chosen).** The customer's world is a storm of scattered needs: a logo here, cards there, a sign somewhere else, each with a red "busy / far / not available" badge. **Freeze.** The cursor clicks the one tile that answers them all, the Elegance Story logo. From then on each need is answered in its own UI window, in four chapters (design · print · products · signs and digital). At the peak, all 12 real images snap into one grid in a single beat: «كلّش تحت سقف واحد».

**Why 3:** it carries the brand's actual promise (one place for everything) as the style's own native moves: *generated accumulation* (the problem), *freeze* (the turn), *light reveal* (the logo) and *the snap grid* (the promise). It also gives the 12 images the client already generated a natural home as the "content" inside the interface.

## 2. Logline and arc

*A storm of scattered printing needs stops dead when one tile, Elegance Story, is clicked, and every need snaps into one grid under one roof in Sétif.*

- **Setup** (0–4 s): disorder. The cards are generated by rule, faster and faster, tilted, with red badges.
- **Turn** (4–5 s): freeze, then digital silence. The cursor's one click.
- **Development** (5–37 s): the logo revealed by light, then four answered chapters, with the giant number «3.20» on its own screen.
- **Peak** (37–40 s): the snap grid. Everything lands on one chord.
- **Ending** (40–45 s): put-away. The tiles retract one per beat until one tile is left lit: the logo, with the contact details. It echoes the single tile clicked at 5 s.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Grammar of a black-stage product reveal**: one idea per screen, light as the reveal tool, a lone giant number. *Learn*: negative space, a single focal element, the number with no caption over it. *Don't take*: any interface, font, melody or shot.
- **Snap-grid launch films**: 1 px lines, everything landing on a beat. *Learn*: typed spawns, curved flights, "the grid is invisible until it fills". *Don't take*: layouts or colours.
- **Steve Reich, *Music for 18 Musicians***: an additive mallet process. *Learn*: build by adding one note per bar, mallets as the pulse. *Don't take*: any melodic material.

## 4. Look

- **Stage**: near-black `#070707` with a cool blue lift and a hairline 48 px grid, as in the style. The neutrals lean blue.
- **Accent**: **brand cyan `#1CCEE9`**. It is the only accent: the cursor, the clicked tile, the "speaking" dot, the confirmations ✓ and the big number. The colour logo brings its own colours; it is the client's image, not UI colour.
- **Problem colour**: red `#FF4D5E`, on badges in the storm only (0–5 s). It never returns after the click.
- **Category codes in motion** (S6 trails only, desaturated): brand blue, purple and magenta.
- **Type**: Cairo 800 for headlines (one line per screen), Cairo 600 for captions in a toast, JetBrains Mono for codes, Inter Tight 600 for the giant «3.20».

## 7. Cue map

120 bpm, 4/4: beat = 0.5 s, bar = 2.0 s. The film is 22.5 bars. A in VCSL marimba range, mode A Dorian (bright, not sad).

| Section | Bars | Music state | Picture hits on notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 storm | 1–2 | **Disorder family**: marimba and vibraphone hard notes at scattered times, detuned ±30 c, random pans, accelerating (the beat divides 1 → 2 → 4 → 8) | each card spawn = one scattered note |
| S2 freeze | 2.5 (4.0–5.0) | **Digital zero** 4.05–4.95 | the click at 5.0 = the first note after silence |
| S3 reveal | 3–4.25 | A bowed vibraphone chord (Am9) swells; one glockenspiel on 5.0 | light sweep 5.4–6.6 |
| S4 design | 5–7 | **Additive marimba process** starts: a 1-note ostinato, plus one note each bar | each tab arrival on a beat |
| S5 print | 7.25–9.5 | + jazz double bass (A, root and fifth), the process continues | sweep starts on a bar line |
| S6 products | 10–12.75 | full pattern; a glockenspiel on each flight landing | 3 landings on beats 1, 3, 5 |
| S7 number | 12.75–14.75 | subtractive: the pattern drops to one note; the digits lock on sixteenths, one marimba note each | dive on a downbeat |
| S8 signs | 14.75–16.75 | the pattern returns, the bass walks | whip on beat 1 |

## 9. Captions and titles

- **Captions = a toast** (the UI component): a dark translucent pill with a 1 px light border and a small cyan "speaking" dot on its right (RTL), Cairo 600 at 40 px, centred on x 500 at y ≈ 1440 (inside the safe zone). It enters with a 0.16 s rise and fade. Hold ≥ max(1.8 s, line + 0.6 s). Until the client's voice arrives, captions follow the script timings.
- **Headlines**: one line per screen, Cairo 800, 76–96 px. A headline and a caption never say the same thing twice on screen at once.
- **The big number** «3.20» has no caption over it (the L7 caption shows only after the number has turned into the facade).
- **The end card** is held for 4.0 s or more.

## 10. Not the demo again (checked after reading DEMO.md)

| | Our demo («Room to Think») | This film | Differs? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structure | calm typed hook → accumulation pull-back → freeze → press → reveal → number → breath → echo | storm from frame 1 → freeze/click → logo → four service chapters → number → snap grid → put-away to an end card | yes (chapters and the snap of real images) |
| Opening | a cursor typing in silence, extreme close-up | a hard storm of request cards from frame 1, Dutch angle | yes |
| Signature shot | the type-stream collapse of 264 UI items | the 12 real service images snapping into one grid on one chord | partly (both are a snap) |
| Camera path | one continuous log pull-back, then a whip | a push through the frozen frame, a lateral track, a top-down plan, a dive into the «0», a whip pull-out between two images | yes |
| Score shape | two phasing marimbas + a synth pad + foley | an additive mallet build (one note per bar) on real instruments only, no SFX, bowed vibraphone as the pad | yes |
| Ending | the cursor finishes its sentence | one tile left → the logo with the contact details | yes |

Five of the six differ.

**Retimings after the reading check:** the headline at 6.5 s, the digits locking at 25.875–26.125, the dive at 27.95, the facade at 28.5, and the L7 and L8 captions moved to fit.

**After the client's voice arrived** (one take, 40.5 s, split into 11 lines at its pauses, silences trimmed only; no speed, pitch or compression change):
- The products section is **one bar longer** (every time after 20 s moves +2 s), so the film is **48.0 s**. Line 5 runs 6.25 s and line 6 runs 4.75 s.
- The picture now lands on the spoken words: preview switches on «لوغو / هوية / الألوان» (10.0, 11.5, 12.5, 13.5), material chips on «زجاج / خشب / جلد / معدن» (16.0, 17.0, 17.5, 18.5), and product landings on «كارطات / ستيكرز / تيشرتات» (21.0, 21.5, 23.5).
- The captions follow the recording: line 4 is «والألوان لي توالم علامتك», line 6 is «مقصوصة على كيفك». Two Whisper models agree on both.
- Voice vs music while speaking: +26.7 dB. Final: −14.0 LUFS, no black frames, all 11 lines transcribe back from the finished film.
