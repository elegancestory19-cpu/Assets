# ELEGANCE STORY — "لوغو واحد… كل بلاصة" (One logo, every surface)

Vertical 9:16 social ad · 1080×1920 · 30 fps · **30.0 s** (27 s film + 3 s end card) · darija voice-over · burned-in Arabic subtitles · −14 LUFS.

Built with the Lemo-Opuscar directing method (DIRECTOR.md): a benchmark, three candidate structures, a shot list, a beat sheet, a cue map, a sound table and reading-time rules. The picture is photoreal clips generated in Google Flow (prompts in `FLOW_PROMPTS.md`). The brand layer (white thread, Arabic type, official logo, end card) and the sound are made in code by our own engine, so the logo and the contact details are always exact.

**Common pattern:**

**What our ad does differently:**
1. It speaks. A darija voice gives the reason to call.
2. One idea carries all 27 s (the logo travelling across surfaces), not a pile of shots.
3. Designed light: a dark stage, the UV glow, gold.

## 1. Benchmark

- **The 13 references (v06, v07, v11, v12 above all):** learn the satisfying process actions (peel, engrave, UV pass, fold) and gloved hands on a clean backdrop. Don't take their footage, client designs, music, watermarks or flash transitions.
- **Premium macro product films (the "match cut on the logo" grammar):** learn to keep the subject in the same place in frame across cuts, so many cuts feel like one shot, plus slow motion on materials and one light source per shot. Don't take any specific film's shots or music.

## 2. Three candidate structures

1. **A day in the workshop.** Dawn to night, machine by machine. Honest, but it is a tour, and tours bore people on social media (v09 shows it).
2. **The client's problem, then the solution.** A blurry logo becomes a perfect print. Strong, but we just used it in the «ماشي غير لوغو» ad, so a repeat would feel like the same ad.
3. **One logo, every surface (chosen).** The same ES logo appears on a card, glass, a sticker, acrylic, gold calligraphy, a stand and a box. It sits in the same place in frame every time, so the cuts read as one continuous take. It ends by pulling back to show all the products together, then the official logo.

**Why 3:**
- The logo is on screen in every shot (your request).
- Each surface is one service, so the services list itself becomes the story.
- The match cut on the logo is the "native move" that only this idea has.

## 3. Logline and arc

**Logline:** A logo that lives on a business card ends up everywhere a business needs it, because Elegance Story knows how to print it on every surface.

- **Setup (0–3 s):** the hook. A UV print head lays the ES logo in gold, and the voice asks «عندك لوغو؟» ("You have a logo?").
- **Journey (3–16.8 s):** seven surfaces, getting faster: card, glass, sticker, acrylic, gold, stand, box.
- **Breath (16.8–17.4 s):** the music stops and one sound of paper remains.
- **Turn (17.4–24 s):** «قولّنا واش تحب…» ("Tell us what you want…"), hands present a fresh print, then the signature pull-back to every product.
- **Ending (24–30 s):** the official logo, the white thread drawing the ES, and the contacts. It bookends the opening: the logo that started on one surface ends as the brand itself.

**Camera moves used:** push, slide, rack focus, locked, tilt, track, orbit, pull-back. That is eight; the method asks for at least four.

## 6. Cue map

- **Tempo and grid:** 100 BPM, 1 beat = 0.6 s, 1 bar = 2.4 s, in 12/8 with a Sétif groove (bendir + darbuka + handclaps) like the «ماشي غير لوغو» ad, so the two ads sound like one brand.
- **Cuts on the grid:** 3.0 · 5.4 · 7.8 · 9.6 · 11.4 · 13.2 · 15.0 · 16.8 · 17.4 · 19.8 · 24.0 · 27.0.
  - The cuts land on beats 5, 9, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25, 28, 29, 33, 40 and 45.
  - The shots shrink from 2.4 s to 1.8 s from S4 onwards: that is the acceleration the method asks for.
  - The breath and the 4.2 s pull-back are the slow parts.
- **Instruments by section:**
  - Hook: low hit, pad, pluck.
  - Journey: groove enters at 3.0; claps from 9.6.
  - Breath: everything out.
  - Turn: keys and pluck only.
  - Pull-back: full groove plus a riser.
  - Logo: hit and full chord.
  - End card: the chord rings, with ticks.
- **Subtitles:** each subtitle appears with its line and holds ≥ max(1.8 s, line + 0.6 s). Where a line is shorter than its window, the subtitle stays until the next line.

| End card | — | A tick per contact line | Chord rings out |

**Sound bridges (J/L cuts):**
- The laser buzz starts 0.2 s before the cut into S3 (J-cut).
- The stand's room tone carries over the cut into S8 (L-cut).

**Mix:**
- Voice around −16 LUFS, with music ≥ 12 dB under every word.
- Master −14 LUFS, true peak −1 dBTP.
- Mute the audio Flow generates on its clips, and rebuild all foley in our mix so the sound stays consistent.

## 8. Titles and subtitles

- **Subtitles:** one line of Arabic per voice line, Cairo ExtraBold 60 px, white on a soft dark band. They sit above y = 1540 px (clear of the TikTok/Reels caption area, which covers the bottom 380 px) and away from the right 140 px (the platform buttons).
- **Accent word:** each line may have one accent word in an ink colour, for example «الزجاج» ("glass") in cyan, «العرس» ("wedding") in magenta, «بالذهب» ("in gold") in yellow and «بأناقة» ("elegantly") in orange.
- **Text inside the AI shots:** no readable text except the logo. Video models garble letters, so any Arabic you see in a shot (the invitation, the calligraphy) is added in post, in Cairo or Tajawal, never generated.
- **Logo:** the full-colour logo (silver ES and wordmark, multicolour ink splash, white circuit lines), as in `logo/ES_logo_3D_splash.png` and `logo/ES_logo_splash_tagline.jpg`. In every shot it is the ingredient you upload to Flow, always in colour; the engraved glass is the one frosted exception, with a colour label beside it. If Flow distorts it, I replace it in post with the real PNG, tracked onto the surface. That is why each prompt keeps the logo flat, facing the camera and in the same place.
